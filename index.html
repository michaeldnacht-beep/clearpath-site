The Mediation Process

From Initial Inquiry to Executed Terms

Each mediation follows a structured process designed to give the parties the best opportunity to resolve the matter in a single session.

  1. Inquiry and Conflict Check

    Counsel provide the names of all parties and counsel. A conflict check is completed before any session is scheduled.

  2. Mediation Agreement

    All parties execute an agreement addressing confidentiality, fees, and the conduct of the session.

  3. Pre-Mediation Submissions

    Each side submits a confidential statement of its position, key documents, and settlement history.

  4. The Session

    The mediator conducts joint and private sessions, tests each position, and facilitates the exchange of proposals.

  5. Settlement Terms

    When agreement is reached, the material terms are reduced to writing and signed before the session concludes.
